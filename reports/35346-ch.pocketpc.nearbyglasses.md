## Notes

- No INTERNET permission; network capture not applicable.
- Scan start/stop works; status text updates correctly ("Scanning active", "Not scanning").
- Foreground service verified: ongoing notification "Background scanning is active" (FOREGROUND_SERVICE | NO_CLEAR flags) posted while scanning.
- Actual smart-glasses detection could NOT be triggered: no BLE devices were advertising in the test environment during the review window (the system's own BLE scanner also reported zero advertisements). BLE scan is genuinely active (registered with the Bluetooth stack); verification of the alert/canary change requires a real smart-glasses beacon (or any BLE advertiser with a matching company ID).
- Permissions: BLUETOOTH_SCAN/BLUETOOTH_CONNECT requested and granted at scan start; location permissions NOT required on Android 13+ (API 36 device) and not requested. App works without location.
- Settings all functional: RSSI threshold dialog (input validated and persisted, changed -75 to -70 and back), canary mode toggle (UI switches between canary and notification mode), log display toggle, debug toggle, cooldown, export log shows correct "nothing to export" toast when log is empty.
- App icon is custom (eyeglasses design), not the default Android icon.
- Privacy posture excellent: no INTERNET, no telemetry, no ads; AGPL-3.0-only; warning screen about not harassing people.
- VirusTotal: 0/67 malicious (undetected).
