## Notes

- Full review pass on OnePlus 13 (API 36): onboarding (privacy choices), app search with local results, app launch from results, web-search prefixes (g / p / dd / bing), in-app browser with Google results, voice-search button, app drawer, app launch from drawer, launcher Settings (wallpapers, custom shortcuts, snippets, OLED, theme, search engine, keyboard autocorrect). No crashes.
- All traffic captured with PCAPdroid (app-filtered). Destinations: Google SERP content (www.google.com, gstatic assets, ogads-pa clients6 = Google ads inside the search page, cdn.ampproject, play.google.com, youtube/ytimg) - loaded by the in-app browser WebView as soon as a query is typed; plus raw.githubusercontent.com = StevenBlack hosts list for the built-in ad blocker (downloaded at browser init).
- Autocomplete suggestions (Google/DDG/YouTube/Bing) and GlitchTip crash reporting are OFF by default (opt-in) and correctly stayed off - no suggestqueries/google, no GlitchTip traffic observed.
- No update check observed; no tracking domains; all endpoints are explained in-app or in source. AntiFeatures (NonFreeNet, Tracking) properly declared in the MR metadata.
- Runtime permissions: READ_MEDIA_IMAGES is requested once at first launch but is skippable (denied, app fully usable); READ_CONTACTS (contact search) and RECORD_AUDIO (voice search) are only requested when the corresponding feature is used - good. QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES + PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS are inherent to a launcher.
- The in-app WebView is the launcher's core search feature, not a decorative author-page webview.
- Custom adaptive launcher icon. MIT license; no accounts, no in-app ads, no tracking.
- VirusTotal: 0 malicious / 0 suspicious / 58 undetected.
